# Candidate components for the IRA CARE watch

Every row is **candidate, to confirm with a hardware engineer**. Each names a part, why it
fits a watch worn 24 hours a day by an older person at home, the key figures, one
alternative, and the manufacturer's page the figures were read from.

**How the figures were checked.** Each figure marked *verified* was read on 24 November 2026
from the manufacturer's own product page or datasheet (fetched as text through this build's
network proxy, after the owner allowed the manufacturers' hosts). Where a manufacturer's site
refused automated access (Analog Devices, ST, NXP, Knowles, Littelfuse/C&K, Omron, Corning,
Panasonic render only in a browser) the row says **not verified** and gives no figure that was
not read from a source; the hardware engineer checks those pages by hand. Nothing in the
software depends on a specific part: the app reads standard Android sensor types
(`docs/hardware/SENSOR_HAL.md`).

## 4. What a hardware engineer does with this list

1. Verify by hand every row marked *not verified*, and re-read the verified ones against the
   full datasheets (several pages give headline figures only: FIFO sizes, low-power currents,
   package drawings).
2. Confirm the compute path: the SW5100 with its own modem and PMW5100 (the simplest board,
   NDA documentation) or an application processor plus the EG915U module (more parts, more
   freedom on AOSP).
3. Decide the dock's wireless-power transmitter: the current-status receivers exist, the
   wearable-class transmitter controllers found are end-of-life, so either a current 5 W
   transmitter part or our own dock controller (the simplest Qi transmitter is a small
   microcontroller with a half-bridge and a coil).
4. Solve the antennas: LTE, GNSS, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth in a 46 mm metal case (a slot antenna in
   the case gap and a tuned ground plane); the single hardest RF problem of the product.
5. Build the engineering prototype on a development board that runs the `aosp` flavour of
   the app (`docs/hardware/SENSOR_HAL.md`, section 5) before any custom board.
