# IRA CARE terms of use

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW.** Written by the build; not reviewed by a lawyer; not to be published
until it has been.

1. **What IRA CARE is.** A companion and alerting service: a watch app, a family app and a
   carer dashboard that check in with a person who lives alone and tell their family and
   carers when something may be wrong. It is provided by the operator named in the privacy
   notice.
2. **What it is not.** IRA CARE is not a medical device, does not diagnose, does not give
   medical advice, and does not contact emergency services. It can fail: a watch can be off
   the wrist, flat or out of signal; a server can be down; a fall can go undetected or be
   detected when there was none. It supports a care circle; it does not replace one. In an
   emergency, call the emergency services yourself.
3. **Accounts and the circle.** Family and carers use accounts the operator issues or invites
   them to. Keep sign-in details private. Only invite people the person has agreed to.
4. **Consent.** What family and carers may see of the person's words and readings is set by
   the person's recorded consent. The operator records it honestly and respects withdrawal.
5. **Your conduct.** Use the service for the care of the person it is set up for. Do not try
   to access other people's data or to defeat its security.
6. **Plans and payment.** Plans and their limits are shown in the app. Prices, billing and
   cancellation terms are the operator's and apply only when the operator has set them;
   nothing is charged until then.
7. **Availability.** The operator aims to keep the service running and tells the circle
   when its monitoring is paused (the dashboard shows it). No uptime is guaranteed.
8. **Liability.** [To be drafted by the operator's lawyer, within what consumer law allows.]
9. **Changes and ending.** The operator may change these terms with notice in the app and may
   end the service with notice; on ending, the person's data is erased as the privacy notice
   says.
10. **Law.** [Governing law and jurisdiction to be set by the operator's lawyer.]
