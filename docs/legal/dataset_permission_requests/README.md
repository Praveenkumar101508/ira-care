on 25 November 2026). Before sending one:

1. Open the official page named under "How to reach them" and confirm the addressee and the

   read at its source from the build environment, and none was copied from a search summary.

3. Check the citation against the page; use the exact format the authors ask for.
4. When a reply arrives, record it in `docs/DATASETS.md` (licence, date, who granted it, any
   conditions) and keep the reply itself with the company's legal records, not in this
   repository.
