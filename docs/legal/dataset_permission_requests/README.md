has been contacted about them. Two **were sent on 26 November 2026** at the founders' instruction,
and replies are awaited: SisFall, to the corresponding author
([`SISFALL_PERMISSION_REQUEST_2026-11-26.md`](../SISFALL_PERMISSION_REQUEST_2026-11-26.md)), and

([`WEDA_FALL_PERMISSION_REQUEST_2026-11-26.md`](../WEDA_FALL_PERMISSION_REQUEST_2026-11-26.md)).

Each file is one request, written from the licence register in `docs/DATASETS.md` (re-verified
on 25 November 2026). Before sending one:

1. Open the official page named under "How to reach them" and confirm the addressee and the
   route (form or email) there. No email address is written in these drafts: none could be
   read at its source from the build environment, and none was copied from a search summary.
2. Replace `[contact email]` with the address the founders want replies to.
3. Check the citation against the page; use the exact format the authors ask for.
4. When a reply arrives, record it in `docs/DATASETS.md` (licence, date, who granted it, any
   conditions) and keep the reply itself with the company's legal records, not in this
   repository.
