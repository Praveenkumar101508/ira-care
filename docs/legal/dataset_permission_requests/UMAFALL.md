## Message

Subject: UMAFall: confirming the licence for use in a commercial fall-detection product

Dear Prof. Casilari and Dr Santoyo-Ramón,

We are IRA CARE, a small company founded by Praveen Kamineti and Sravani Perni. We are building
a care product for older people who live at home: a wrist-worn watch that alerts the people
who look after them when it detects a fall.

UMAFall's wrist-worn sensor makes it valuable for evaluating a wrist fall detector. Before we
use it, we would like to confirm its licence: version 8 on figshare appears to be released
under CC BY 4.0, while a University of Málaga repository record for UMAFall lists
Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0. We have not downloaded the dataset.

**What we would do with it**

- Train and evaluate a small fall-detection model for a wrist-worn device, using UMAFall's
  wrist recordings, and measure it on participants it was not trained on.
- Only the trained model (a few thousand numeric weights) would ship in the product. No
  UMAFall recording, or any part of one, would be included in the product or published.

**How we would handle the data**

- No redistribution, in whole or in part; no upload to any public service or repository.
- Stored only on the company's own encrypted storage, accessible to the people working on the
  model, and kept out of our source repository.
- Deleted, with confirmation to you, whenever you ask.
- No attempt to identify any participant.

**Citation**

In every publication, model card, technical document and product notice that describes the
model, we will cite the figshare record with its version DOI
(doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.4214283.v8) and the UMAFall paper, in the form you prefer.

**What we are asking**

1. Which licence applies to UMAFall version 8: CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-NC 4.0?
2. If it is non-commercial, may we use it, on the terms above, to train or evaluate a model in
   a commercial product?

Thank you for publishing UMAFall.

Yours sincerely,

Praveen Kamineti and Sravani Perni
